# Character arc

**Status: locked.** Names and flavour texts are final, pending the name and brand checks under Open. The app update is request #2 in `claude-communication.md`. Animation seeds are starting points for the briefs and can still evolve there.

One pig character per XP level, shown on the trading card. Each card is one joke: name, flavour text and animation work together. The animation ideas are seeds for the briefs, not the briefs themselves.

## Progression

| Levels | Band | The joke | The motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Clueless | Failure, confusion | Off-balance, wobbly holds, things go wrong |
| 5–6 | Keen amateur | A habit or an attitude | Eager, sloppy timing |
| 7–8 | Competent | A skill overdone | Strong, snappy |
| 9–10 | Pro | Swagger | Precise, confident holds, the cleanest loops |

## Decided

- **Visitors are level 1:** the visitor's locked card shows Raquete emprestada, the same character as a real level-1 player. Today the app gives visitors their own character (`XP_VISITOR_INFO`) — changing that is part of the hard-lock request

- **Level 1 wears trainers** instead of hooves (the "sapatilhas da Vans" in his flavour text) — the only exception to character rule 6. Generic trainers, no logo or signature pattern; swap pieces for both legs

## Open

- **Level 10 name uses a real athlete's name** (Roger Federer). Check before hard lock whether using it in the app is OK — a well-known person's name in a product can raise image-rights and trademark questions. Fallback: Lenda do bairro
- **Levels 1, 3 and 7 name real brands** (Vans, Lacoste, Mercadona). Same check as level 10 before hard lock; a light mention is usually lower risk than a person's name

- **Props beyond racket and ball:** a phone (3)
- **Effects** some levels need beyond props: a camera flash (3), backspin lines (7), speed lines (8), possibly a sweat drop — for the props library
